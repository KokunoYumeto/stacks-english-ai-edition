# Contributing evidence and corrections

This is an independently maintained, AI-produced derivative of the Stacks
Project. The official upstream contribution process is documented by the
[Stacks Project](https://github.com/stacks/stacks-project); this repository is
not an upstream review channel.

Useful reports against this edition are narrow and reproducible. Include:

- the exact file, label or tag, and source commit;
- the smallest relevant old and proposed replacement span;
- a mathematical justification or counterexample;
- any affected references, formulas, diagrams, or build targets; and
- enough information to distinguish a source error from a convention or style
  preference.

Candidate corrections are expected to preserve upstream attribution and
licensing, stable identifiers, exact source maps, append-only adjudication,
deterministic checks, and explicit non-endorsement language. Lexical similarity
alone is not evidence of mathematical equivalence.
