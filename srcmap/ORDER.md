# Integration order

## 1. FAC

FAC is first. It is directly cited by the current Modules, Cohomology of
Schemes, and Curves chapters and supplies the coherent-sheaf foundation on
which GAGA depends. Its public target inventory has 661 stable units through
EOF. This makes it both the highest-leverage and the best-bounded first pass.

## 2. Tôhoku

Tôhoku is second. The current Stacks tree cites it directly in Cohomology of
Sheaves and Injectives, and its 990-node source graph is already complete.
Most units are expected to map to existing stronger modern infrastructure;
genuine gaps and historical hypothesis defects remain eligible for new text
or issue records.

## 3. GAGA

GAGA is third. Its algebraic, formal, and comparison consequences are in
scope, but the current Stacks tree does not develop a general category of
complex analytic spaces. The analytic core must therefore be classified
honestly as outside current scope or as conditional comparison material; it
must not be forced into an algebraic chapter. GAGA follows FAC so every
coherent-algebraic input can reuse the FAC crosswalk.
